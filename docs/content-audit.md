# Content Audit — Piano Migration

Audited 5 August 2026 against the 33 manuscript files and 29 chapter example pairs present before the piano prototype.

## Findings

- Parts I–III contain 26 single-concept chapters; Part IV contains three integration chapters; four back-matter chapters synthesize the original Role/Motion/Groove model.
- Every numbered legacy chapter embeds bass-clef-only ABC and printed bass tab. Important harmonic claims therefore depend on prose rather than audible context.
- The repeated Dm7–G7–Cmaj7 laboratory provides useful control but the accompaniment is absent. Its three-bar shape also displaced the promised 8–16-bar musical studies.
- The old Role taxonomy overlaps with the new architecture: Anchor maps to Ground; Colorist is too vague and becomes Reframer; Connector and Voice-Leader belong primarily to Motion; Shadow and Commentator become Interaction behaviours.
- Groove chapters 19 and 25 contradict the new notation boundary by calling written eighth-note displacement “pocket,” “push,” or “lay-back.” They require full rewrites, not line edits.
- Published prose exposes repository paths, implementation tags, draft status, and tab-source commentary.

## Inventory summary

| Area | Chapters | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Part I — legacy Role | 9 | 1 retained as historical source, 6 rewritten/redistributed, 2 merged |
| Part II — Harmonic Motion | 9 | all rewritten or merged into the new Motion/Role sequence |
| Part III — Groove | 8 | 2 removed as definitions, 6 rewritten/merged |
| Part IV — Integration | 3 | rewritten into Interaction and Design |
| Back matter | 4 | merged into Design, glossary, and exercises |

The per-file decision is authoritative in [migration-matrix.md](migration-matrix.md). **The Ground**, **The Definer**, and **The Inverter** now form the published Role pilot. Replaced legacy examples remain migration sources.

## Redundancy and contradictions

- Anchor, Root Motion, and several integration passages repeat grounding/root-function explanations.
- Connector, Passing Motion, Voice-Leader, and Approach Note overlap without a stable boundary between route, event, and role.
- Colorist and Substituted Root both describe bass-register reharmonization without consistently distinguishing inversion, extension, and a genuinely different chord.
- Pocket, Push/Lay-Back, and Syncopation conflate notation with performed microtiming.
- The boilerplate laboratory explanation and tab-source note recur in nearly every chapter.

## Acceptance result

Every manuscript file has an explicit decision in the migration matrix. No legacy chapter is approved for publication unchanged.
